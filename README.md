# Overview

This repository contains code, processed datasets, and supporting materials for data science and computational research conducted in pediatric and neonatal medicine at Johns Hopkins Children's Center. Our research primarily focuses on neonatal health and morbidities associated with prematurity, with an emphasis on neonatal lung disease, including bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD), as well as necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC) and other complications affecting preterm infants.

Our work integrates bioinformatics, clinical informatics, statistical modeling, and meta-analytic approaches to investigate disease-associated molecular signatures, characterize underlying biological processes, and identify patterns that may improve our understanding of neonatal and pediatric disease. Although lung disease and complications of prematurity are central areas of interest, this repository also encompasses research into diseases affecting other organ systems across neonatal and pediatric populations. 

Each project is organized in its own directory, with project-specific documentation describing its research objectives, data sources, analytical methods, and reproducibility requirements.
